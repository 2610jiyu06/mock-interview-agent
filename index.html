How to Configure Real AI (Google Gemini - Free Tier)

  1. Visit Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Get API key and copy your key.
  3. Open the .env file in your project folder (created alongside .env.example).
  4. Paste your key: 
    GEMINI_API_KEY=AIzaSyYourGeneratedApiKeyHere
  5. Restart the Flask server (python app.py). The badge will automatically turn green to indicate AI-Powered Mode!
